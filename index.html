ABOUT NAZAQAT

Where Tradition Meets Modern Elegance

NAZAQAT is a Pakistani women's fashion brand created to celebrate elegance, individuality and the timeless beauty of traditional Pakistani clothing.

Our collections bring together graceful colours, carefully considered details and timeless silhouettes, creating pieces for women who appreciate the beauty of Pakistani fashion while embracing a refined contemporary style.

At the heart of NAZAQAT is a simple philosophy: elegance is found in the details. From the selection of colours and patterns to the overall character of each ensemble, our aim is to create designs that feel distinctive, graceful and effortless to wear.

Our journey begins with Gul Rukh Volume 1, the first collection from NAZAQAT. Inspired by florals, expressive colours and refined Pakistani aesthetics, Gul Rukh introduces eight individual designs, each carrying its own personality and character.

From soft romantic shades to rich and expressive tones, NAZAQAT celebrates the confidence, femininity and elegance that make Pakistani fashion so distinctive.
