ABOUT NAZAQAT

Where Tradition Meets Elegance

NAZAQAT is a Pakistani stitched fashion brand created around elegance, femininity and timeless style. Our collections bring together carefully selected colours, graceful patterns and beautiful detailing to create outfits designed for women who appreciate traditional Pakistani fashion with a refined, contemporary touch.

Our Gul Rukh Edition 1 celebrates individuality through a collection of stitched ensembles, each with its own distinctive character. From soft romantic shades to rich statement colours, every design is selected to bring confidence and elegance to your wardrobe.

At NAZAQAT, we believe that true style is found in the smallest details. Every collection reflects our passion for beautiful Pakistani fashion and our commitment to creating outfits that feel graceful, distinctive and effortless.
